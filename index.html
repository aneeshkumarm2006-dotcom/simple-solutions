Questions people ask

Answered plainly.

What is final expense life insurance?

Life insurance meant to cover funeral costs and other end-of-life expenses. The policies are deliberately small and simple compared with a full life insurance policy, which is why they are easier to qualify for.

Do I need a medical exam?

Many plans do not require one. Some ask a short set of health questions instead, and some of those policies include a graded death benefit during an initial period — meaning a limited amount is paid rather than the full benefit if death occurs in that window. Ask the agent to confirm before you apply.

Who is eligible?

Generally U.S. citizens and legal residents ages 50 to 80. Final eligibility is set by the carrier and by the state you live in, and not all plans are available in all states.

How much does it cost?

Premiums depend on the coverage amount, your personal qualifications, and the carrier and state. A licensed agent can price it for your situation on the call.

How is the benefit paid out?

As a lump sum to the beneficiary you name on the policy. They can use it for funeral and burial costs, medical bills, outstanding debts, or anything else — it is not restricted to funeral services.

Is this a government program?

No. This is an advertisement for life insurance from a licensed insurance agency. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any government agency or state burial program.
