Common Questions

What is final expense life insurance?

Final expense life insurance is a type of coverage intended to help with funeral costs and other end-of-life expenses. Coverage options are designed to be clear, straightforward, and easy to understand.

Do I need a medical exam?

Many plans offer an easy application process with no medical exams required. Some policies may require health questions and may include a graded death benefit during an initial period.

What is a graded death benefit?

Some policies pay a limited benefit if death occurs during an initial period after the policy starts, rather than the full coverage amount. Whether this applies depends on the carrier, the plan, and your answers to any health questions. A licensed agent can explain the terms of any plan you are considering.

Who is eligible?

These plans are generally available to U.S. citizens and legal residents ages 50–80. Coverage options vary by individual eligibility, carrier, and state.

How much does it cost?

Premiums depend on coverage amount, personal qualifications, and may vary by carrier and state. A licensed insurance agent can review pricing with you based on your situation.

Can my coverage be cancelled or my rate raised?

Policy terms differ by carrier and plan. Ask the licensed agent to confirm whether the plan you are considering has level premiums and how long coverage lasts before you apply.

Is coverage available in my state?

Not all plans are available in all states. A licensed insurance agent can confirm which options are offered where you live.

How is the benefit paid out?

The benefit is paid to the beneficiary you name on the policy. They can use it for funeral costs or any other expense — it is not restricted to funeral services.
