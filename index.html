Questions people ask

Answered plainly.

What is final expense life insurance?

It is life insurance meant to cover funeral costs and other end-of-life expenses. The policies are deliberately small and simple compared to a full life insurance policy, which is why they are easier to qualify for.

Do I need a medical exam?

Many plans do not require one. Some ask a short set of health questions instead, and some of those policies include a graded death benefit during an initial period.

What is a graded death benefit?

If a policy has one, it pays a limited amount rather than the full benefit if death occurs during an initial period after the policy starts. Whether it applies depends on the carrier, the plan, and your answers to any health questions. Ask the agent to confirm this before you apply.

Who is eligible?

Generally U.S. citizens and legal residents ages 50 to 80. Final eligibility is set by the carrier and by the state you live in.

How much does it cost?

Premiums depend on the coverage amount, your personal qualifications, and the carrier and state. A licensed agent can price it for your situation on the call.

Can my rate go up or my coverage be cancelled?

That depends on the plan. Ask the agent whether the policy you are considering has level premiums and how long the coverage lasts, and get the answer before you apply.

Is coverage available in my state?

Not all plans are available in all states. The agent will confirm which ones can be written where you live.

How is the benefit paid out?

It goes to the beneficiary you name on the policy, as a lump sum. They can spend it on anything — it is not restricted to funeral services.
